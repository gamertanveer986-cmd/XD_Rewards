# XD Rewards back 🏆

📱 XD REWARDS — App Plan (Short Version)

XD Rewards is a watch-to-earn platform where users watch ads and earn real money. Users get ₹0.05–₹0.10 per ad, ₹5 per referral, and ₹10 signup bonus (non-withdrawable). Minimum withdrawal is ₹50 with auto UPI payout (1–5 mins).

App login options: Email, Phone, Google with mandatory email verification. Until verification, earning and withdrawal are locked. The app includes Dashboard, Wallet, Leaderboard, and Support.

Leaderboard auto refreshes every 24 hours. Security includes one device-one account, fraud detection, and anti-skip ad protection.

UI theme is Black + Red premium with animated login showing live earnings. Admin panel manages users, ads, payouts, leaderboard, and fraud monitoring.
